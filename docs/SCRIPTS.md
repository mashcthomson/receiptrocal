# Recording scripts

The authoritative presentation and product-demo scripts are in [SUBMIT.md](../SUBMIT.md). Use those to avoid duplicate versions drifting apart.

Record the presentation for 2 to 3 minutes and the product demo for no more than 3 minutes. The demonstration should show the synthetic label, one-lamport mismatch, receipt reuse rejection, evidence export and the merchant's 402/200/409 flow.

Capture the browser app window with appshot on the review machine. Do not record unrelated windows, account pages or credentials. No uploads or public posting are authorized in this run. The operator supplies actual recording links when submitting.

Do not narrate a successful live payment match unless that exact result was observed. The current evidence proves RPC retrieval; it does not prove a customer invoice was paid. Preserve unsupported, unavailable and network-error outcomes honestly.
